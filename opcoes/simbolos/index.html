Four rooms: olive, dove, eye, sea.

Finnish-Palestinian photographer, artist and visual reporter whose experimental lens moves between documentary and fictional storytelling. Her work is organised by what it is about, not by when it was made.

The four symbols are the ones Nora uses to describe herself — 🫒🕊️🧿🌊. Move over a room to see what lives inside; on a phone, they stack.
