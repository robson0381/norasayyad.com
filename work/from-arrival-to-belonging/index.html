From Arrival to Belonging? A Decade in Portraits

Refugee lives shaped by 2015 and 2022, and the policies and borders in between.

From Arrival to Belonging? A Decade in Portraits traces refugee lives shaped by two moments — 2015 (Middle East) and 2022 (Ukraine) — and by the policies, borders, and double standards in between. The project honours resilience, skills, and labour, while asking who gets to belong in Finland and its workforce — and at what cost.
