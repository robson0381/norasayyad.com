# Institutional marks

The Home credential wall is intentionally implemented with monochrome typographic wordmarks in the prototype.

Groups:

- **Published:** The Washington Post, The Times
- **Exhibited:** Helsinki City Museum, The Finnish Museum of Photography
- **Collected:** Migration Institute of Finland, The National Library of Finland

## Production rule

Before launch, replace a typographic wordmark with an official SVG/PNG only when the asset comes from the institution itself (or its official media/brand kit) and its use is permitted.

Do not scrape or redraw institutional logos from random image-search results.

The layout is already asset-agnostic: official logos can be dropped into the same credential cells without changing the Home architecture.

## Visual treatment

- monochrome preferred;
- equal optical height rather than equal pixel dimensions;
- preserve original logo proportions;
- no arbitrary recolouring if brand rules prohibit it;
- provide accessible `alt`/labels;
- keep generous whitespace so the block reads as cultural/editorial credibility rather than sponsorship.
